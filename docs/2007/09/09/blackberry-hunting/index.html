Blackberry Hunting

September 9, 2007

Yesterday we took a bike ride down the Springwater Trail in search of blackberries. Unfortunately it’s the end of the season and most of the reachable berries had been picked over. We ended up getting a small bowl full, which we intend to put on cheesecake.
